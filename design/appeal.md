# Appel à l'App Review Board — Agenda Le Romandie (4.2.2)

## Où soumettre l'appel
- Dans le **Resolution Center**, la décision de refus propose un lien **« Submit an Appeal »**.
- Sinon, directement : https://developer.apple.com/contact/app-store/?topic=appeal
- Choisis « Guideline 4.2.2 », référence la **Submission ID** et la version **1.0 (6)**.
- **Joins la vidéo de démo** (voir plus bas) ou un lien vers celle-ci.

---

## Texte de l'appel (à coller — anglais)

Dear App Review Board,

We are appealing the rejection of “Agenda Le Romandie” (v1.0, build 6) under Guideline 4.2.2.
We believe the app was under-evaluated, and we’d like to respectfully provide context.

1) The review was performed on an iPad Air (M3). The app is designed for iPhone, and its
   primary native feature — a Home Screen Widget — is not available on the iPad Home Screen.
   As a result, the app’s core functionality could not be experienced during review. We
   kindly request that the app be reviewed on an iPhone. We have attached a short demo video
   recorded on iPhone that shows each native feature in action.

2) The app is not a web-viewing experience. It uses several native iOS frameworks to deliver
   functionality a website cannot provide:
   • WidgetKit + App Intents: Home Screen widgets in three sizes with interactive pagination
     buttons and deep links that open a specific concert in the app.
   • PDFKit + on-device storage: users import their PDF ticket (received by email) and the app
     stores it locally and displays the QR code offline — usable at the venue with no network.
   • UserNotifications: favorites schedule local reminder notifications before each concert.
   • EventKit: add a concert to the system Calendar with an alarm.
   • MapKit: an in-app venue map with directions (Apple Maps / Google Maps).
   • A native interactive month Calendar view, plus search, filtering, a share sheet, and
     offline access to cached data. The app is organized into two tabs (Agenda / Calendar).

3) Many comparable apps that surface event or venue information — and that provide native
   widgets, notifications, and offline features — are available on the App Store. We believe
   this app meets the same bar for native, lasting functionality.

We are committed to a high-quality experience and are happy to make any specific change you
can point to. Thank you very much for reconsidering the app on an iPhone.

Kind regards,
Diane Delallee

## Script de la vidéo de démo (30–60 s, filmée SUR TON IPHONE)

Active l'**Enregistrement d'écran** (Centre de contrôle), puis :

1. Écran d'accueil : montre le **widget** (moyen ou grand) → appuie sur **◀ ▶** pour changer
   de page → **tape un concert** → l'app s'ouvre sur SA fiche.
2. Sur la fiche : **cœur** (favori) → montre l'alerte de notification / mentionne le rappel.
3. **Mon billet** : « Ajouter mon billet (PDF) » → choisis un PDF → le **QR s'affiche**
   (dis « accessible hors-ligne »). Optionnel : passe en **mode Avion** et rouvre le billet.
4. **Carte** → « Itinéraire ».
5. Onglet **Calendrier** → tape un jour avec un point rouge.

Arrête l'enregistrement. La vidéo est dans Photos → tu peux la joindre à l'appel (ou la
déposer sur un lien : iCloud, YouTube non répertorié…).

Astuce : garde-la courte, nette, sans temps mort. C'est LA preuve que le testeur iPad n'a
pas pu voir.
